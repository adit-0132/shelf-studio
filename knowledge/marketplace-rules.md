# Marketplace field and evidence notes

Verified/reviewed 6 September 2026. Shelf Studio creates drafts for review and export; it does not publish listings or certify marketplace acceptance. Current seller-account category fields take precedence over this snapshot.

## Amazon India: title and highlights

Amazon India's News_Amazon announcement says non-media titles move to a 75-character maximum, with a separate 125-character Item Highlights field, effective 27 July 2026. The app enforces those maxima for its Amazon fields. Its longer catalogue title is a separate deliverable for the seller's catalogue and must not be pasted into the shorter Amazon field.

Source: [Amazon India title update](https://sellercentral.amazon.in/seller-forums/discussions/t/cd6a14da-a5c7-4f62-b886-7ead97407b3c), official staff announcement, page read.

Amazon India's general listing page still mentions 200 characters. This is a documented conflict between a broad older guide and the dated newer announcement; the app uses the newer rule. Observed long competitor titles may be legacy data and are not a reason to disregard that announcement.

Source: [Amazon.in listing overview](https://sell.amazon.in/sell-online/list-your-products), official overview, page read. Do not use its 200-character value as the current title default.

## Other writing fields

Amazon's listing guide describes five feature bullets, description and images as separate elements. Amazon staff guidance on the India forum gives up to 500 characters per bullet and 2,000 for a product description. The app uses these as conservative default ceilings. The long catalogue description is a separate expanded draft, not a way to bypass an Amazon field limit. A+ layout and module eligibility are separate from writing a long text draft.

Sources: [Amazon listing components](https://sell.amazon.com/blog/amazon-product-listings), official guide, page read; [Amazon India staff listing guidance](https://sellercentral.amazon.in/seller-forums/discussions/t/5b82dff2-813d-4d17-a298-6a5332edfa9a), staff reply, page read. Treat the latter as broad guidance, not an exhaustive category schema.

The 2025 title announcement discouraged repeated words and a specific set of special symbols. The app checks for these as review warnings. Use ordinary, readable punctuation and avoid all-caps promotional language.

Source: [Amazon 2025 title announcement](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/533f9cf7-3b5e-4974-b523-02e4a1a42c5f), historical policy background. Its older title-length limit has been superseded for this workflow.

## Images

Amazon's photography guide recommends a white-background product shot, accurate appearance, clear focus and dominant product framing. Shelf Studio preserves a clean white setting plus four lifestyle images. The white setting is the natural candidate for a main-image review. Decorative props belong to lifestyle scenes and must not imply included accessories. AI-generated detail fidelity and the live category requirements still need the seller's review; a generated white image is not automatically a certified-compliant main image.

Source: [Amazon product photography guide](https://sell.amazon.com/blog/product-photos), official guidance, page read.

## Flipkart

Flipkart's public seller page describes a listing as title, description, images and other product information, with dimensions, features and usage instructions for new products. In this research environment its indexed content was accessible while direct text opens of the seller page returned a minimal shell. The source is therefore indexed evidence, not a full seller-console schema. No universal Flipkart title or description length is claimed here.

Source: [Flipkart listing overview](https://seller.flipkart.com/sell-online), official site, indexed text.

## Originality and accuracy

Competitor pages are observations of merchandising choices, not evidence for this product's specifications and not text to reuse. Some titles and attribute tables disagree. Record discrepancies; never resolve them by choosing the more flattering claim. Avoid copied descriptions, competitor brand terms, unverifiable safety/performance claims, price/stock promises and invented certification or origin stories.

## Updating this snapshot

External research is manually refreshed. When a new rule is verified, update this file, the source ledger and any corresponding validation limit in src/listing.mjs. Guide content is hashed into a version and frozen in each generation job. New product entries expand the stored catalogue; only seller-marked style examples enter future writing context. Do not silently treat generated copy as verified training evidence.
